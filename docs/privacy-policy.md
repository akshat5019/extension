# Resume Assist privacy policy

Resume Assist helps you fill job application forms using a resume profile you review and answers you provide.

## Information processed

The extension reads PDF, DOCX and TXT resume files you select and extracts readable text locally in your browser. It can process contact details, education, skills, employment information and any other information present in that document. The original file and extracted text are not saved by the extension.

The profile you review and save is retained in your browser's local extension storage. It is not synchronized through browser sync, and it is not encrypted by this extension. Answers supplied for missing questions on an individual application are not saved in the extension's persistent storage.

## Information shared

Resume Assist has no developer-operated backend, analytics, or external AI processing service. It does not send resumes or profiles to the extension developer.

When you ask it to fill a job application, the filled information becomes available to that website. If you enable automatic submission for an application, the extension can click its final Submit button after its completeness checks and cancellable countdown. The receiving website's privacy policy governs its handling of your application. You can attach documents and provide consent directly on that website.

## Control and deletion

You can review and edit your profile before use. Delete my saved data removes the profile from the extension's local storage. You can cancel automatic submission from the page panel or extension popup. Deleting local data does not delete applications or information previously shared with job websites.

## Permissions

The extension requests activeTab for temporary access to the page you choose, scripting to scan/fill that page and monitor optional submission, and storage for your saved profile. It does not request access to every website in the background.
